clvkit brand manual

Rules for the kit family: clvkit today, segmentationkit and others later. Everything here renders from the files in this directory, so the page cannot drift from the assets. Each rule states the measurement behind it. Change the rule here before changing an SVG.

The system

Brackets wrap the invariant

Product names vary; kit is what repeats. Bracketing the constant gives every product one shared token, so the suite reads as a suite. Bracketing the product name instead would force [segmentation] next, and no two marks would share anything.

Brackets never appear in anything typed

The package is clvkit. The logo may say what a terminal cannot. Two concrete reasons, both verifiable in seconds:

TypedWhat it actually means
pip install clv[kit]the kit extra of a package named clv, per PEP 508
ls clv[kit]a shell character class: matches clvi, clvk, clvt. Never clvkit.

The lockup

Symbol then wordmark, symbol at 72 and wordmark at 64 in height. The symbol is the family and is never redrawn per product; only the word beside it changes.

Clear space

Keep the height of the symbol's corner radius, 56 units at the 256 viewBox, clear on every side of the lockup. Nothing crosses it.

The family

One symbol, one tracking value, one colour scheme. Only the word changes. segmentation[kit] is the stress case: it is the longest name planned, so any typographic change has to be judged on it rather than on clv[kit].

Colour

Two purples, not one. They are stepped so each separates from its own background by the same amount: 6.12:1 and 6.14:1. Reusing a single purple across both modes drops one side to 3.1:1, under the 4.5:1 threshold.

clv and the brackets #4F46E56.12:1 #7C83FF6.14:1

The ring on the symbol #FFFFFF6.29:1 on plate #FFFFFF3.21:1 on plate

The ring is inset, never outset. An outset white border disappears against a white page, which is the default background of both GitHub and PyPI.

The favicon

The symbol at quarter scale, so the two files cannot drift apart. Two deliberate departures from a straight scale-down: the plate is full-bleed, because browsers and launchers apply their own mask, and the ring is stroke 3.5 rather than the scaled 2.5, since a hairline is the first thing to vanish at 16px.

Do not

  • Do not move the brackets onto the product name. [clv]kit breaks the family.
  • Do not put brackets in a package name, an import, a CLI flag or a URL.
  • Do not colour kit in the brand purple. Purple marks clv and the brackets; kit is ink.
  • Do not reuse one purple across both modes. That is the 3.1:1 case.
  • Do not move the ring outside the plate.
  • Do not set the brackets as text in a new asset. Draw them.
  • Do not tighten the tracking to make a short name look sharper. It is judged on the longest name in the family.
  • Do not add a stroke, shadow, gradient or glow. The plate, the ring and the glyph are the whole mark.
